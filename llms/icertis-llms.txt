# Icertis

> Icertis is an AI-native contract intelligence company. Its Contract Intelligence (ICI) platform turns enterprise contracts into structured, actionable data across the full agreement lifecycle — authoring, negotiation, execution, obligation management, and analytics — for sell-side, buy-side, and corporate contracts. AI capabilities include the "Vera" assistant and agent-powered drafting, redlining, and negotiation. This is an API Evangelist network profile enriched from Icertis' public web presence; Icertis publishes no public developer portal or OpenAPI (platform APIs are documented behind customer authentication).
